# Third-party data

KanjiPop's dictionary (`data/dict.db`) is generated from files by the Electronic Dictionary Research and
Development Group (EDRDG), used under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence:

- **JMdict** (word meanings and readings) - https://www.edrdg.org/jmdict/j_jmdict.html
- **KANJIDIC2** (kanji readings and meanings) - https://www.edrdg.org/wiki/index.php/KANJIDIC_Project

- **Word frequency ranking** - the global frequency list from [Jiten](https://jiten.moe/frequency-dictionaries),
  licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. It is only used to order results (most common word first).

See https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html. The generated database is a derivative work and is
distributed under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 terms.
